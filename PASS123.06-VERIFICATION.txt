PASS 123.06 - MOBILE HERO VIDEO PLAY ZONE

Mobile-only hero media behavior:
- Long-form and short-form hero cards show their thumbnail while approaching the center play zone.
- A card starts its muted inline loop after it enters the center play zone.
- At most one long-form and one short-form card play at the same time on mobile.
- Cards unload back to their thumbnail after leaving the play zone.
- Save-Data, slow-network, hidden-tab, offscreen-hero, and reduced-motion safeguards remain active.
- Desktop hero playback behavior remains unchanged.
- Retains Pass 123.05 mobile hero composition, Pass 123.04 mobile review auto + swipe, and all prior release fixes.
