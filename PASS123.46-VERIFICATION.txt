PASS 123.46 — MOBILE REVIEW MODAL CONTINUITY

Goal
- On mobile, opening a review must NOT restart or pause the "Words from people I've edited for" carousel.
- The review rail must keep moving continuously behind the review modal.
- Closing the review must reveal the rail at the position it naturally reached while the modal was open, then continue normally.

Implementation
- The mobile requestAnimationFrame autoplay effect is now mounted once for the lifetime of ClientReviews.
- Review modal state no longer tears down/recreates the carousel runtime.
- Existing swipe synchronization, seamless loop normalization, reduced-motion handling, modal scroll lock, and focus restoration are retained.

- package: 9.13.46
